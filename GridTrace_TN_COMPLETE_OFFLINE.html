R&D METHOD

How GridTrace works

01 · Detect
Match synchronized outages and restoration patterns across consumer data.
02 · Map
Infer consumer → transformer relationships using correlation, geography and billing behavior.
03 · Quantify
Compare transformer input energy with billed energy and outage behavior.
04 · Explain
Separate likely technical loss, metering signals and commercial-risk indicators for investigation.

Demo disclaimer

All figures, outage events, transformer values and district metrics in this prototype are synthetic demonstration data. They are not live TANGEDCO/TNPDCL operational figures and should not be treated as official utility records.
